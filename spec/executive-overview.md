# Executive overview / 老板看板

Read-only company-wide reporting for the verified owner, retaining OneLaser Certia, the 32/16/13/12 type scale, red/neutral palette, bilingual controls and light/dark theme.

Show company OKR progress (equal company objectives, equal valid KRs within each), accepted/total task counts, awaiting review and overdue counts. Compare departments by recorded active members, tasks, acceptance, overdue and departmental OKRs. List follow-up items and company goals. Missing metrics are unknown, not zero; costs and trends remain absent without verified source data. All-time recorded scope is explicit, with snapshot time. Overdue and waiting categories overlap.

The static site offers a clearly fictional Company owner identity and an executive entry; it does not authenticate anyone. Live mode renders a server-authorized summary only for `/me.executiveAccess`, backed by an explicit internal-user-ID allowlist. No employee/manager role can self-upgrade; existing task permissions are unchanged. Salary, dismissal and automatic personnel conclusions are out of scope. DingTalk/Grok activation remains a separate requirement.
